## Why it was re-scanned

The wrist D435i is mounted **upside down**, and the hand-eye mount was
recalibrated on 2026-09-03 against the 180°-corrected stream
(`cams/cellmap/calib/tool0_to_camera_2026-09-03.yaml`, two tags, independent
agreement 8.38 mm / 1.06°). `cell-2026-08-28` was built on the older
2026-08-27 mount, so every surface in it is offset by that change.

Built from `/wrist/color_upright/*` + `/wrist/aligned_depth_upright/*`
(frame `wrist_color_upright_optical_frame`, mirrored principal point
cx 416.55 / cy 248.83). Captain freedrive sweep, ~15 min, arm masked out by

## Verification (`cams/cellmap/tools/verify_map.py`)

**Tag anchors — the check that matters most.** The two AprilTag centres were
measured by the camera during the same day's hand-eye calibration, in
`base_link`, *without using the map*. The map puts real surface there:

| tag | measured centre (m) | nearest mapped surface | surface height vs tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| tag 0 | +0.1157, −1.0578, −0.0565 | **11.1 mm** | 4 mm |
| tag 1 | −0.5057, −1.0575, −0.0572 | **11.3 mm** | 5 mm |

A board is ~3 mm thick and a voxel is 20 mm, so ~10 mm *is* agreement. This is
the end-to-end check: joint states → mount extrinsic → depth → integration. A
wrong mount displaces the map bodily and would fail here immediately.

**Table height.** Dominant horizontal plane at **z = −0.070..−0.060 m**, 11 mm
from the independently measured tag boards — *identical* to what
`cell-2026-08-28` gave. The table did not move and neither did the floor of the
map, which is the strongest evidence that the new mount did not introduce a
systematic shift.

**The arm is absent — the segmenter worked.** Nearest mapped point to each live
link origin, all far outside the 0.06–0.08 m link radii:

| link | nearest mapped point |
|---|---|
| shoulder_link | 0.478 m |
| upper_arm_link | 0.478 m |
| forearm_link | 0.452 m |
| wrist_1_link | 0.325 m |
| wrist_2_link | 0.320 m |
| wrist_3_link | 0.322 m |
| tool0 | 0.322 m |

**Vertical structure — READ THIS BEFORE TRUSTING THE FAR FIELD.** Faces beyond
y 0.8 run from 1.28 m to 1.94 m with the densest at **1.64–1.68 m**.
`cell-2026-08-28` had its shelf front at 1.36–1.52 m (matching the approach
demo's measured 1.516 m) and the wall behind at 1.96–1.98 m. Today's
distribution does **not** line up with that, and the honest reading is that the
far field is *different*, not that it is verified: the cell contents were moved
between the two scans, and this sweep covered the room more widely. The table
and tag checks above pin the near field, which is what the arm works in; treat
anything beyond y ~1.3 as "what the camera happened to see today".

## Known gaps

Coverage **cannot** be read off `map_ctl.py esdf` — see the comment there.
`esdf_and_gradients_unobserved_value` is +2.0 so unseen space reads as FREE to
cuMotion, and `esdf_integrator_max_distance_m` is also 2.0, making unobserved
and "≥ 2 m clear" indistinguishable in that query (37 % of voxels sit at that
ceiling, being some mixture of the two). **Unobserved is treated as free**, so
this map protects only against what the camera actually saw. The cell must be
physically clear of anything that was not scanned. Re-scan after moving
anything reachable (`RUNBOOK.md` §10).
