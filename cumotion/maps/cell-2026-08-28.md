# cell-2026-08-28 — first real cell map

**The map file is NOT in this repo.** At 74.9 MB it is over the ~50 MB commit
threshold, so it lives on the workstation:

```
~/ur10/cumotion/maps/cell-2026-08-28.nvblx   74.9 MB   (map, load this)
~/ur10/cumotion/maps/cell-2026-08-28.ply     18.1 MB   (mesh, for inspection/renders)
```

`start_cumotion_map.sh` searches `cumotion/maps/` and `~/ur10/cumotion/maps/`,
newest first, so it finds this without arguments.

Scanned 2026-08-28 by hand-guided freedrive sweep of the wrist D435i, on the
hand-eye calibration of 2026-08-27 (`cams/cellmap/calib/tool0_to_camera_2026-08-27.yaml`,
validated to 3.8 mm / 0.59 deg median — see `cams/cellmap/README.md`).

## Verification

**Table height.** Dominant horizontal plane at **z = -0.070..-0.060 m**. The two
AprilTag boards on that table were measured independently by camera the day
before at z = -0.053 / -0.054 m; the 1-2 cm difference is the board thickness
plus one voxel, so the map and the tags agree.

**Shelf / gondola.** Vertical structure beyond y 0.8 concentrates at
**y = 1.36..1.52 m** (peak 1.44), consistent with the approach demo's measured
shelf front of 1.516 m at x ~ 0. A second, denser face at **y = 1.96..1.98 m**
is the wall/back behind it.

**The arm is absent — the segmenter worked.** Nearest mapped point to each of
the arm's live link origins:

| link | nearest mapped point | link radius |
|---|---|---|
| forearm_link | 0.473 m | 0.075 |
| wrist_1_link | 0.600 m | 0.070 |
| wrist_2_link | 0.674 m | 0.070 |
| wrist_3_link | 0.691 m | 0.070 |
| tool0 | 0.691 m | 0.060 |

Only 432 of 198,236 points fall in the 0.35 m radius column above z 0.35 where
the arm body sweeps. The 632 points within 0.20 m of the shoulder origin span
z -0.063..0.230 m: that is the **table surface and the robot's mounting
pedestal**, which are static fixtures and *should* be in the map.

## Known gaps

25 % of the workspace box is unobserved, and **unobserved is treated as free**
by cuMotion — the map protects only against what the camera saw. That is
expected for a one-pass sweep bounded by the arm's reach; re-scan after moving
anything reachable (`RUNBOOK.md` §10).
